How it works

Three small steps to your forecast.

From sign-up to a personalised prediction in under a minute.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up with your name, email and a password. We'll email you a 6-digit code to confirm it's really you.

  2. 2

    Share your habits

    Slide in your attendance, weekly study hours, previous CGPA, quiz scores and class participation.

  3. 3

    Get your forecast

    Our trained model estimates your final GPA on a 4.0 scale — along with a friendly note on what it means.

Under the hood

Simple on the surface, solid underneath.

Predictions come from a scikit-learn Random Forest pipeline trained on student performance data. Accounts are protected with SHA-256 hashing and SMTP one-time codes.

Explore the source on GitHub
Pipeline architecture final_gpa_model.pkl
Imputer
SimpleImputer(strategy='mean')
Feature scaler
MinMaxScaler(feature_range=(0, 1))
Estimator
RandomForestRegressor(n_estimators=100)
Target output
Clipped continuous scale [0.00, 4.00]
Auth protocol
SHA-256 digest + 6-digit SMTP OTP
